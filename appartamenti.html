Check-in and out

Check-in is available from 3:00 pm. Self check-in with a digital key lets you arrive whenever suits you best.

A valid ID or passport for every guest, as required by Italian law. You can upload it online before arrival.

A refundable pre-authorisation may be requested on your card and released within a few days after check-out.

Price and cancellation

Choose your apartment, select your dates and book online in a few clicks, or contact us directly by phone or email.

Yes, reservations can be modified or cancelled from your confirmation email, according to the selected rate.

Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Non-refundable rates offer a lower price.

The main guest must be at least 18 years old.
